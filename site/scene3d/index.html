Environment controls · Scene

Scene and location

Drag to orbit. Right drag to pan. Wheel to zoom. On touch screens, drag with one finger to orbit; use two fingers to pan or zoom.

Use Location to choose a hospital, floor, and room. Click a building or floor in the scene to enter it. Views switches between hospital, floor, and walk views. The toolbar header can be dragged to move it.

Nursing cube

Use Patient → Inspect nursing cube to focus it. Drag the cube or use the arrow buttons to rotate. Drag the control panel by its header to move it. Click or tap a face, or use the numbered floor buttons, to select a floor. Right-click a patient room on a cube face to enlarge its waveforms across that face; right-click again to restore all six rooms. Selection never travels.

The current floor face is color inverted. Another floor keeps its normal colors until selected, when it receives a teal highlight. Use Visit Floor to travel. Resume rotation clears the selection. Close inspection returns to the floor view.

Walk mode

Choose Views → Walk mode, then click the scene for mouse look. W/A/S/D moves, Shift moves faster, E opens the elevator, and [ / ] changes the tablet patient. Keys 1–7 toggle the tablet speakers from left to right; 0 mutes all. Press I to inspect or lower the tablet. You can also click or tap its sound buttons after opening it. Click the scene outside the tablet to resume mouse look; Esc releases the mouse.

On touch screens, use the toolbar to choose rooms and views.

Unit walk tour

Use Motion → Play unit walk to play or pause. Drag its timeline to seek. Views → Floor view returns to the overview. Motion also opens or closes the elevator.

Patients and waveforms

Patient arrows move between rooms or tablet patients. Inspect tablet opens its view in walk mode and frees the cursor. Its header has speaker buttons for each available waveform: 1 ECG II, 2 ART, 3 CVP, 4 PAP, 5 pressure, 6 flow, and 7 calculated volume. Press the same number again to mute that speaker; 0 mutes all. You can also click or tap the buttons. Changing rooms mutes every speaker. Vital Waveforms and Ventilator Waveforms open the available full recordings for the selected patient.

The full waveform view has Real-Time Feed and Review Feed when that source is available. Use Close to return to the scene.
