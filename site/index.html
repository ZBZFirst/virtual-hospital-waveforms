    Display and measurements

    Observational playback and optional musical sonification only. Node colors are visual signal mappings, not clinical alarms. The atlas samples synchronized source values at 10 Hz; opened waveform charts and derived breath measures use the full 50 Hz feed.

    PIFR is maximum positive flow; PEFR is the magnitude of minimum negative flow. Peak pressure is the maximum pressure sample. PEEP is estimated from the final five pressure samples when expiration is present. MAP is the time-weighted mean airway pressure over a complete sampled breath, not mean arterial pressure.

    Calculated volume integrates signed flow in L/min within each marked breath; positive flow is inspiration. VTi and VTe sum positive and negative portions separately. Breath-relative volume resets at each breath start; VTi minus VTe is retained for completed breaths. Missing or invalid intervals have no derived breath measures.

    Timing and identity

    Most time axes are estimated from numeric sample index at 50 Hz; the one timestamped recording uses its sample timestamps for integration. There is no change detection, clinical interpretation, or live patient connection. Unit and room names are virtual assignments, not real hospital locations. The displayed Patient ID is the filename prefix; it does not confirm patient identity. Date-like filenames are shifted and do not establish a shared patient timeline. Mode words are filename annotations, not a measured mode channel.

    Source

    Room waveform perspectives

    ZeBeZo cards show observational measures calculated from loaded 50 Hz samples after a completed BS/BE breath. Hahnicity cards show experimental JavaScript ports of selected ventMAP metadata, ventparliament mechanics, and PVA heuristic calculations. They run in this browser; Python repository functions are not executed on the server. A card can lag while analysis runs. Neither perspective changes waveform playback. This is not a clinical tool.
